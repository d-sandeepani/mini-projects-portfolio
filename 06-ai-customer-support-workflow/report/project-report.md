# AI Customer Support Workflow Project Report

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates a basic AI-assisted customer support workflow for a fictional online business named NovaCart. The workflow uses AI to classify customer messages, draft support responses, identify potential risks, and prepare outputs for human review.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Classify customer messages into support categories.
- Generate professional draft responses.
- Identify missing information and potential risks.
- Organize workflow results in CSV files.
- Document human-review requirements, findings, and limitations.

## 3. Tools Used

- ChatGPT for AI-assisted classification and response drafting.
- CSV files for organizing customer messages and workflow results.
- Markdown files for prompts, procedures, checklists, and reports.
- Screenshots for project evidence.

## 4. Workflow Summary

1. Created a sample dataset containing 10 fictional customer messages.
2. Classified the messages into support categories.
3. Compared predicted categories with expected categories.
4. Recorded classification results in the dataset.
5. Created a prompt for generating customer support responses.
6. Generated draft responses and identified missing information or risks.
7. Prepared a human-review checklist and preliminary review notes.
8. Recorded workflow information in a tracking CSV.
9. Documented the process, findings, limitations, and evidence.

## 5. Classification Results

The AI-predicted categories matched the expected categories for all 10 messages in this sample dataset.

- Messages evaluated: 10
- Matching classifications: 10
- Observed sample accuracy: 100%

This result applies only to the small fictional dataset used in this project. It does not demonstrate 100% accuracy in real-world customer support situations.

## 6. Response Generation

AI-generated drafts were prepared for the sample customer messages. The drafts were intended to be professional, polite, and helpful.

Potential risks and missing information were recorded where relevant. The drafts should not be sent to customers without appropriate human verification.

## 7. Human Review

A human-review checklist and preliminary review results were documented. Unless a reviewer has actually checked and approved each response, the review status must remain Pending.

The preliminary notes are not evidence that a completed human review took place.

## 8. Limitations

- The dataset contains only 10 fictional messages.
- The sample does not represent the full variety of real customer requests.
- The observed classification result cannot establish real-world performance.
- AI-generated responses may contain errors or omit important details.
- Order, refund, delivery, and other customer-specific information must be verified before responding.
- Human review remains necessary before sending responses to customers.

## 9. Conclusion

This project demonstrates a documented, AI-assisted customer support workflow using a small fictional dataset. It includes message classification, response drafting, risk identification, workflow tracking, human-review documentation, and project evidence.

The project provides a foundation for further testing with a larger and more diverse dataset.
