## Reviewer Notes

These findings are preliminary assessments of the wording only. They do not confirm compliance with an actual company's policies.

Before marking a draft as approved, the reviewer must read the complete response, check accuracy, privacy, tone, and suitability, and confirm that it does not make unsupported promises.

The fictional examples have not been sent to customers. No real customer accounts or business systems were accessed.

## Project Limitation

The dataset contains only 10 fictional customer messages. These results cannot establish how well the workflow would perform on real customer requests.
