# NovaCart — Human Review Checklist

## Project Information
Project: AI-Assisted Customer Support Workflow  
Business: Fictional NovaCart  
Purpose: Review AI-generated customer support response drafts before use.

## Review Checklist

For each AI-generated draft, check the following:

1. Accuracy
- Does the response use only the information provided?
- Does it avoid inventing order status, tracking information, delivery dates, refund approvals, or product details?

2. Customer Privacy
- Does it avoid asking for passwords, full payment card details, or unnecessary personal information?
- Does it direct account-specific checks to an authorized support channel when needed?

3. Tone and Professionalism
- Is the response polite, friendly, clear, and empathetic?
- Does it acknowledge the customer's concern appropriately?

4. Missing Information
- Does the response identify information needed to investigate the issue?
- Does it ask reasonable clarification questions when necessary?

5. Business Policy and Promises
- Does it avoid promising refunds, replacements, compensation, or completed actions without verification?
- Does it avoid claiming access to systems or information that the AI does not have?

6. Human Review Decision
- Approve: The draft is suitable for the next stage, subject to normal business procedures.
- Revise: The draft needs changes before it can be used.
- Escalate: The issue requires an authorized support agent or specialist.
- Pending: The review has not been completed.

## Important Limitations

- This project uses fictional customer messages and a fictional business.
- The review does not establish real-world business performance.
- AI-generated responses must not be sent automatically.
- A human reviewer must verify the response before any real customer use.
- No real orders, refunds, deliveries, or customer accounts were accessed.
